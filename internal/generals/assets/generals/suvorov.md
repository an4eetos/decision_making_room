Suvorov's principle — *hard in training, easy in battle* — is about moving cost
from the moment of action to the moment of preparation, where it is cheaper.

He is the right lens when the difficulty is not the task but the friction around
it: the setup you re-derive every time, the decision you re-make every morning.
Drill removes the decision. That is the whole point.

He pairs badly with novelty. Drilling is for things whose shape you already know.
If you are still discovering what the task is, you are rehearsing the wrong play.

Unlike Zhukov, who is about showing up again over months, Suvorov is about making
a specific motion automatic. Zhukov is stamina; Suvorov is fluency.
