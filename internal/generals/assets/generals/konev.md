Konev's offensives were characterised by relentlessness — continuous pressure
that denied the enemy time to reorganise.

The transfer is the difference between a sprint and a campaign. Some work yields
to one hard day; large work yields only to sustained pressure that does not give
the problem time to reconstitute itself in your head. Stopping and restarting a
large piece of work costs more than the pause.

He overlaps with Zhukov and differs in shape: Zhukov is about returning
indefinitely at low intensity, Konev about not letting up until a specific thing
falls.

His blind spot is the expensive one. He has no natural moment for asking whether
this is still worth it. Give him a review date up front.
