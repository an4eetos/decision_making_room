At Myeongnyang, Yi Sun-sin fought a vastly larger fleet in a strait whose
currents he understood and they did not. The victory was decided by the choice of
water, not by the fighting.

The transfer is choosing the ground. When you cannot win the general contest,
the move is to make the specific contest happen somewhere your one advantage is
decisive — and to prepare that ground before engaging.

He is the natural lens for a solo project against established competition, or for
work under real constraints.

His blind spot is a flattering one: underdog framing is emotionally comfortable.
If you actually have the resources, this lens will still tell you a clever story
about why you do not.
