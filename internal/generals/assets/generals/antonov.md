Antonov ran Soviet operational planning and was known for briefings that were
short, exact and free of performance — a rare thing in that system, where being
impressive was often safer than being clear.

The transfer is that a situation you cannot state in three sentences is usually
one you have not finished thinking about. The compression is the work, not the
write-up.

He is the natural lens for a pre-mortem, a written decision, or any moment where
you are about to explain something to another person — including yourself next
month.

His blind spot matters on genuinely ambiguous problems: forced clarity there
produces a summary that is clean and wrong.
