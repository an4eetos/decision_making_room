Kutuzov gave up Moscow rather than fight for it, on the grounds that the army
mattered and the city did not — and was proved right when winter did what battle
could not.

The transfer is that not every position is worth defending and not every moment
is the moment. The instinct under pressure is to act, and acting is often how a
recoverable situation becomes an unrecoverable one.

He is the natural lens for an irreversible decision and for anything where you
feel pushed. He is the wrong lens for a stalled task, where his counsel is
indistinguishable from the avoidance already happening.

The blind spot is severe and symmetrical to Patton's. Between the two you get a
real argument, which is the point of running more than one.
