Rommel commanded forward, on the grounds that the situation at the front was
never what the map behind it said.

The transfer is the distinction he forces: has the *objective* changed, or only
the *route*? Most plans that feel broken need a new route and keep the objective.
Confusing the two is how projects get abandoned at the first obstacle, and also
how people grind on at an objective that stopped mattering.

He is an operational lens, not an endorsement — see `docs/generals.md`.

His blind spot pairs badly with a restless mind: he always has a reason to
change. If you have already revised twice this week, that is Zhukov's cue, not
his.
