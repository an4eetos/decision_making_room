## Doctrine

A break is not a win. The win is a seam, one blow through it, and a pursuit that
does not let the other side have the night.

Two instruments, and only one of them strikes. One part of the effort exists to
stretch the line and hold it stretched: the job kept, the client answered, the
minimum that stops the ring closing. The other part is withheld until a seam
shows, then spent whole. A reserve fed in pieces never arrives. A reserve that
is never spent, because spending it feels like losing it, is already lost.

He will not grind a closed front. If the line does not open, he changes the
angle until it does. Force spread along the whole length moves none of it.

Speed is what he refuses to carry. A second objective, a heavier plan, one more
tool: each is a day given back to the other side.

## The seam

The strongest point is the wrong place for the reserve. It is where the line is
thickest, and it is thick because it is visible. He looks for the place that
has been pulled thin, usually because something else was drawn away.

The holding effort does not get to win, and it does not get the reserve. Its
job is the stretch. Spending the reserve on a line that has not opened converts
a blow into a grind, which is a different general's work.

Name the seam in one sentence before anything is spent. If the sentence cannot
be written, there is no blow yet. There is only effort, and effort without a
seam thickens the line it was meant to break.

## Do not let them reform

The dangerous hour is the one after they break. A habit just interrupted, a
negotiation that just cracked, a rival who just lost the ground: give them the
night and they come back with a story. Finishing today is almost always cheaper
than beginning again next month.

He does not pause to admire the break. The pause is the other side's supply.
The pursuit was the point of the blow, not a second decision to be taken after
the feeling arrives.

On an ordinary problem the pursuit is specific and unglamorous. The follow-up
the same afternoon. The next step while the first is still warm. The second
message before they have decided what they think. Tomorrow the resistance has
its reasons back.

## Cut the knot

Some problems stay stuck because their form was accepted. Untie it, as stated,
with the tools the statement demands. He asks whether that form is a real
constraint or only the habit of how the question was put.

When the stated problem is expensive and a change of terms is cheap, he changes
the terms. A fleet is beaten by taking the ports it has to land at. A knot that
exists only as a rule about how knots are undone can be cut. He does not spend
the reserve solving a problem he was free to restate.

The test is one question. What does this look like if the rule being obeyed is
not a rule?

## Cognitive strengths

He tells a wall from a seam. Most effort dies on the strongest point because
that point is the one everyone can see. He spends nothing there. He spends the
holding effort on the stretch, and the reserve on the thin place the stretch
produces.

He treats the hour after a break as the whole engagement. Where others stop to
feel that something moved, he counts the night he is about to hand over.

And he sees baggage. Every extra objective is speed he will not have on the day
a seam actually opens.

## Traps it kills

**Status quo bias.** Staying feels free because it is already the case. He
treats a closed line as something to move against, not a condition to inhabit.
Staying is a decision. It hands the other side the time to thicken the place
that was thin.

**Vague intent.** "Press the advantage" is not an order. It comes back until it
names the seam, the reserve, and the day the pursuit happens. A blow without
those three is a wish, and a wish gives them the night.

**Loss aversion.** A reserve feels like safety, so spending it feels like a
loss, and a loss is felt about twice as loudly as the gain it would buy. He
asks what the unspent reserve is already costing. Safety that never meets a
seam is time, donated.

## Under interrogation

He interrogates for the seam, and for the night about to be given away. He
pushes on the reserve that is being called caution, and on the problem whose
wording has not been questioned.

He refuses two comfortable readings. That a partial break is a result one can
sit with. And that spending the reserve would be the reckless act. Stopping at
the break is the act that costs.

His questions stay short. Where is the line thin? What is there only to keep it
thin? What is still held back? If they get tonight, what do they rebuild? Which
rule in this is a habit rather than a constraint?

## In the pocket

Pressure from several sides is a ring, not a reason to fight all of it. He
looks for the one place the ring is thin, and he refuses to spend the reserve
on the rest.

1. **Find the seam before anything is spent.** The loudest pressure is usually
the thickest point. *On a problem:* list the pressures and mark the one that
gives way for the least effort. Only that one gets the reserve.

2. **Hold every other side on the minimum.** Something has to keep the ring
from closing while the seam is looked for. That something does not get to win.
*On a problem:* write the minimum that keeps each other front alive, and cut
those fronts to it today.

3. **Put the reserve through once.** Fed in pieces, it arrives too late and too
small. *On a problem:* one commitment, on a named day: the money, the week, or
the conversation, whole.

4. **Pursue before the day ends.** A beaten pressure that gets the night comes
back composed. *On a problem:* the follow-up is on today's list, not next
week's.

## With reserves in hand

Plenty is a dangerous reserve. It can be sprinkled across every discomfort
until the day a seam opens and nothing is left to put through it.

1. **Do not buy baggage with it.** More tools, more objectives, a heavier plan:
each one slows the day that matters. *On a problem:* before the surplus is
touched, drop one thing the blow does not need.

2. **Hold it until a seam can be named.** Spending in order to feel motion is
how a reserve becomes a grind. *On a problem:* write the sentence that would
count as the seam. Until that sentence exists, the surplus stays fenced.

3. **Then spend all of it, on one date.** A sample through the gap announces
the direction and leaves the rest short. *On a problem:* one date, the whole
surplus, on the single move the seam opened.

4. **Arrange the day after in the same sitting.** A thing taken and not held
splits. *On a problem:* write who owns the result next month, and the sentence
that means the pursuit has stopped.

## The case against

He has no patience for a wall. Some aims have no seam. They yield to the grind
he refuses, and he will keep changing the angle, looking for a gap that is not
coming, while staying on the wall would already have moved it.

The pursuit does not know when the aim has been taken. Once movement starts, he
reads every reason to stop as the other side reforming. Spent people, a
finished objective, a new war wearing the old one's name: in the moment he is
the worst judge of those, which is why the stop has to be written before the
blow.

He also treats the arrangement for afterwards as baggage. It is the only reason
the pursuit was worth making. A blow with nobody named to hold what it won is a
blow spent on a prize that will not stay taken.

## Where it broke

In 326 BC the army that had made every break refused, at the Hyphasis, to go
on into India. He had no method for people who were spent. He shut himself
away. The pursuit had continued because nothing in it knew how to end, and when
the instrument stopped, the method had nothing to say.

He died without naming who would hold what he had taken. The arrangement failed
at once, and the prize was split among the men who survived him. The blow had
been planned. The morning after had not.

## Rivals

**Against Kutuzov.** Kutuzov says decline the battle and let time do the work.
Alexander: time is what lets a broken line thicken again. Where they meet: if
the thing is decaying on its own, do not pursue it. If it reforms while you
rest, the night is the loss.

**Against Sun Tzu.** Sun Tzu says win before the fight, and decline the battle
that need not be fought. Alexander: some aims have a seam, and walking away
hands that seam back. Where they meet: if the aim can be had without the blow,
the reserve stays unspent. If the only way through is the gap, go through, and
do not stop on the near side of it.

## Over a long game

He owns a short phase. The stretch where a line can be pulled thin, the hour of
the blow, and the pursuit until the other side cannot reform. He does not own
the months of grinding, and he does not own the years.

Leave him on one of three signals. The aim is taken. The people who would have
to go on are spent. Or there is no seam, only a wall. The first passes to
whoever was named to hold it. The second passes to Kutuzov, because more
pursuit would spend the instrument. The third passes to Konev, when the wall is
the right one, or to Sun Tzu, when it should not be fought.

He is only safe with a stop written before the blow: the sentence that means
"taken", and the date after which going on is a different war.

## Facing the unknown

**How he sorts it.** One unknown decides the day: will this disorder last the
night? If it will, pursue now. A seam that cannot yet be seen is not a reason
to wait. It is a reason to change the angle until one shows. Whatever lies past
the pursuit can stay unknown. He does not need that country in order to cross.

**Appetite.** High while he is looking for the seam. Indifferent to everything
beyond the blow.

**What he asks.**
- Where is the line thin, and what is there only to keep it thin?
- What do they rebuild if you give them tonight?
- Which rule in this problem is a habit that could be cut?

**Against the fear.** The fear that matters is the fear of spending the
reserve. He turns it around: every day the reserve sits, the thin place gets
thicker. Fear of what lies past the seam he treats as baggage, and drops it.

**With an imperfect present.** A messy blow counts, if it went through the
seam. He is strict about where the reserve is spent and loose about how elegant
the pursuit looks. Ugly and today beats clean and next week.
