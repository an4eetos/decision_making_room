Zhukov's method was mass and return: accept that the cost is high, plan for it,
and keep showing up until the weight tells.

The honest version for ordinary work is that compounding beats intensity, and
that the main threat to compounding is not failure but the broken streak treated
as a verdict. One block today plus one tomorrow beats one heroic day and then
nothing.

He is the right lens for anything measured in months. He is the wrong one when
the question is whether the front is worth holding at all — that is Sun Tzu or
Kutuzov, and Zhukov will never raise it himself.

Fatigue is information to him, not weakness: reduce the scope, keep the slot.
