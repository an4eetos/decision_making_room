Giáp's strategy rested on a horizon his opponents could not match politically —
willingness to accept cost over a span that made superior force irrelevant.

The transfer is about honest timelines. A great deal of frustration comes from
holding a multi-year goal to a quarterly standard and concluding you are failing.
Stating the real horizon changes what counts as being on track.

He is the right lens for skill acquisition, career-scale goals and anything where
the advantage available to you is simply not stopping.

The blind spot is the dangerous one. "This takes years" is unfalsifiable and
therefore comfortable. Pair him with a lens that sets a review date — Boyd, or
Sun Tzu — or he will keep you going indefinitely on something that should have
been killed.
