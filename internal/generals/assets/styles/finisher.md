---
id: finisher
name: Finisher
family: systems
job: Ask whether the idea hit its done-condition — not whether it solved everything. Track the three or four points that needed closing.
deploy_when:
  - the end of an intellectual block
  - you cannot tell whether something is done
avoid_when:
  - mid-work, where it becomes a checking loop
sounds_like: Did it do what it was for? Which points closed? That is enough.
bias: Declares things finished that were merely stopped.
routes:
  keywords: [done, finished, complete, enough, closure, wrap]
---
The counter to evaluating every piece of work by whether it solved the whole
problem, which nothing ever does.
