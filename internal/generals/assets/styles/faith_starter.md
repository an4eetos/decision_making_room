---
id: faith_starter
name: Faith Starter
family: contact
job: Move on a simple working story without auditing it first. The story does not have to be proven, only useful enough to act on.
deploy_when:
  - starting cold with high anxiety
  - every step is triggering a check
avoid_when:
  - the cost of being wrong is high and irreversible
sounds_like: Assume it works. Go. You can find out it does not in twenty minutes.
bias: Skips verification that was actually warranted.
routes:
  keywords: [anxious, overthinking, verify, check, doubt, cold start]
---
The point is not optimism, it is deferring proof until after contact. Most
verification before starting is a way of not starting.
