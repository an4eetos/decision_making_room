---
id: gut_check
name: Gut Check
family: scouting
job: Give the intuition ten seconds before any mechanism starts. Verify afterwards, if at all.
deploy_when:
  - several viable paths and no clear criteria
  - early scoping, before the shape is known
avoid_when:
  - you have no experience in the domain to have intuition from
sounds_like: Ten seconds, no reasoning. Which one? Now we check whether that holds.
bias: Confuses familiarity with correctness.
routes:
  keywords: [intuition, gut, feel, instinct, sense, which one]
---
The order matters. Mechanism first kills the gut read before it speaks, and the
gut read is often carrying real pattern knowledge you cannot articulate.
