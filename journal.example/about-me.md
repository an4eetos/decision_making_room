# About me

This file and everything under `context/` is injected into every answer. Your
`daily/` notes and anything else in this folder is retrieved on demand instead.

Write as much as you want here — there is no size cap in the code. If it grows
past a few thousand words, move the stable parts into `context/` and keep the
day-to-day in `daily/`.

## Who I am

- Replace this with a few lines about your work and what you are building.
- What you are optimising for right now.

## How to talk to me

- Be concise and specific. Skip the preamble.
- Challenge a bad idea rather than agreeing with it.
- Say "I don't know" when the context doesn't cover it.

## Don't suggest

- Things you have already ruled out, listed here so you stop re-litigating them.
