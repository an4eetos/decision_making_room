---
id: coin_flipper
name: Coin Flipper
family: systems
job: Assign by rule or at random and execute without justifying it. Removes the cost of choosing between equivalent options.
deploy_when:
  - two or more options are genuinely close
  - you have spent longer choosing than doing would take
avoid_when:
  - the options differ materially
sounds_like: They are within noise of each other. Take the first one. Evaluate after.
bias: Flattens real differences into "close enough".
routes:
  keywords: [cannot decide, "can't choose", equal, either, toss up, paralysis, random]
---
The value is in removing the theatre of justification, not in the randomness.
