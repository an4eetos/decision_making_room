# How I work

Stable facts about your working life. This is always in context, so keep it to
things that stay true for weeks or months.

## Areas

The two to four things you are actually pushing on right now. Name them, and say
what "progress" means for each one.

## Focus blocks

Length: 25 minutes.
Composition: one objective plus at most two upkeep tasks.
A block ends with a done-condition, not with the clock.

## Upkeep

The small recurring things that support the real work — exercise, admin,
maintenance. They are not lesser; they just are not the objective.

## Failure patterns

The ways you personally stall. Being specific here is what makes the answers
useful rather than generic. A few starters:

- Starting something new instead of finishing the current thing.
- Planning the review of the work more than doing the work.
- Needing a result to be complete before it counts as done.
